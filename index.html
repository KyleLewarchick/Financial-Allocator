Behavioral Financial Allocator

This interactive tool helps you plan how to allocate your discretionary income between Investing, Debt repayment, and Savings. It combines financial calculations with behavioral considerations, aiming to balance long-term net worth optimization with your current needs and priorities.

💡 What makes this different: Most investment tools only help you decide what to invest in (stocks vs bonds). We help you decide how much to allocate between investing, debt, and savings based on your full financial picture and risk tolerance.
🎯 Recommended workflow:
  1. Fill out your financial info, estimate your Risk Tolerance, Debt Aversion, and set your priorities below
  2. Click Run Projection to see your initial allocation
  3. Click Risk Assessment to take a quick 4-question quiz for a more accurate risk score
  4. Your Risk Tolerance will automatically update, giving you an optimized allocation AND personalized investment portfolio
