🚀 How to Get Started (for beginners)

  1. Open a brokerage account — Popular options: Fidelity, Charles Schwab, or Vanguard (all free, no minimums)
  2. Link your bank account — Connect your checking account to transfer money
  3. Deposit funds — Transfer your monthly investment amount
  4. Search for the ETF — Use the ticker symbol (e.g., "VTI") in the search bar
  5. Buy shares — Enter dollar amount or number of shares, then click "Buy" (most brokers allow fractional shares)
  6. Set up auto-invest — Many brokers let you automate monthly purchases
