🚀 How to Get Started

  1. Open a brokerage account — Fidelity, Schwab, or Vanguard (all free)
  2. Link your bank — Connect your checking account
  3. Search for ETFs — Use ticker symbols (e.g., "VTI")
  4. Buy shares — Most brokers allow fractional shares
  5. Set up auto-invest — Automate monthly purchases
💰 Pro tip: Consider a Roth IRA if eligible — tax-free growth! 2024/2025 limit: $7,000/year.
